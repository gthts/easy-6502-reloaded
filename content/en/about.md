# About Easy 6502 Reloaded

Easy 6502 Reloaded is a learning and development environment for the assembly language of the 6502 processor. It consists of a tutorial, a source code editor, an emulator with execution view, monitor and breakpoints, and a reference.

## Your data

Everything runs in your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no requests to third-party servers. Source code, quick slots, settings and reading progress are kept in the browser storage of this device and are not sent anywhere. With “Save file” you keep your program yourself.
